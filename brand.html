Identity

The Frazier & Co. mark.

A geometric F in bone, followed by three ascending bars in gold — the campaign curve the firm is hired to produce. The mark reads at 16px in a browser tab and at billboard scale without redrawing.

Palette

Ink, bone, gold.

Deep navy carries the weight, bone keeps it readable, and a single gold accent marks the things that matter — numbers, actions and the growth curve in the logo. No second accent colour.

Do

  • Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the monogram
  • Use the dark lockup on ink, photography and video
  • Use the monogram alone for avatars, favicons and app icons
  • Keep gold for accents — it should never carry body copy

Don't

  • Recolour, rotate or stretch the mark
  • Place the dark lockup on a busy or light image without a scrim
  • Set the wordmark below 120px wide — use the monogram instead
  • Add effects: drop shadows, outlines, gradients over the type

Type

Fraunces for voice, Inter for clarity.

Headlines are set in Fraunces — a serif with enough character to read as a firm with a history. Everything operational is Inter: interface, tables, dashboards and long copy.

Fraunces 600

Headlines, statistics, the wordmark. Tight tracking (-0.015em), italic reserved for emphasis and the ampersand.

Inter 400 / 500 / 600

Body copy, navigation, forms, labels and every number that appears in a report. Uppercase with 0.18em tracking for eyebrows and labels.
